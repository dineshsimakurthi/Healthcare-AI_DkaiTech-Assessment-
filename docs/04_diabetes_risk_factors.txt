Risk Factors for Type 2 Diabetes
Based on public guidance from CDC and NIDDK (summarised for this demo).

Factors that raise the risk of type 2 diabetes:
- Being overweight or obese, especially extra fat around the waist
- Physical inactivity
- Family history: a parent or sibling with diabetes
- Age: risk rises from about 35 to 45 years onwards
- High blood pressure (130/80 mmHg or higher) and abnormal cholesterol or triglycerides
- History of gestational diabetes, or giving birth to a baby weighing more than 4 kg
- Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)
- Prediabetes
- Certain ethnic backgrounds, including South Asian, African, Hispanic and Indigenous populations, have higher risk
- Poor diet high in sugary drinks, refined carbohydrates and processed meat
- Smoking

Some risk factors such as age and family history cannot be changed, but weight, diet, activity and smoking can.
