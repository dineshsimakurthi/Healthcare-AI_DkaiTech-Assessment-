Lifestyle Changes to Prevent and Manage Diabetes
Based on public guidance from WHO, CDC and NIDDK (summarised for this demo).

Healthy eating:
- Fill half of the plate with non-starchy vegetables, a quarter with lean protein and a quarter with whole grains.
- Choose whole grains, pulses, nuts, fruit and vegetables over refined grains such as white bread and white rice.
- Avoid sugary drinks such as soda and packaged juices, and limit sweets and fried food.
- Eat regular meals and watch portion sizes.

Physical activity:
- Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week, such as brisk walking, spread over most days.
- Add muscle-strengthening exercise two or more days a week.
- Avoid sitting for long periods; take short walking breaks.

Weight: losing 5 to 7 percent of body weight can reduce the risk of developing type 2 diabetes in people with prediabetes by more than half, according to the US Diabetes Prevention Program study.

Other habits: stop smoking, limit alcohol, sleep 7 to 9 hours, and manage stress. People with diabetes should check blood glucose as advised, take medicines as prescribed, and have regular check-ups.
